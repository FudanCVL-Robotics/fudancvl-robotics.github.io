Source leaderboard ↗
Five dimensions, one overall score +

WBench measures video quality, setting adherence, interaction adherence, consistency, and physics compliance. Overall scores are copied from the public leaderboard; displayed rounded dimensions are not used to recalculate them.

Choose the right evaluation split +

Navigation covers 158 cases with 19 metrics. The full benchmark covers 289 cases with 22 metrics, adding subject actions, event edits, and perspective switches. Compare scores within the same split and judge.

Original and beta judges +

Doubao-Seed-2.0-lite is the original VLM judge. Qwen3.8-27B is a self-hosted beta judge, available for a subset of navigation submissions. The judge affects scene adherence, subject adherence, and physics fidelity. Beta results form a separate ranking.

Submission sources and coverage +

“Self-reported” identifies a self-evaluation submission. “Mixed evaluation” identifies self-evaluation with organizer VLM evaluation. Unspecified sources remain unspecified. Open model details to inspect provenance and the sample count for each metric. Model variants count as separate entries.
