WBench++: A Broader View Of World-Model Capability

More ways to interact. More ways to understand what works.

Explore the idea ↓All research ↗

A benchmark is most useful when it reveals what to improve next. An overall score can help summarize progress, but the explanation lives in the tasks, the interaction history, and the patterns behind individual failures.

Broader coverage, clearer questions

Different interactions challenge different parts of a world model. Navigation asks for coherent space. Editing asks a model to change one thing while preserving others. Subject actions introduce intent, timing, and causal structure.

This project preview frames WBench++ as a direction for studying those capabilities together. Its proposed emphasis is diagnostic breadth: connecting an evaluation result to the kind of interaction that produced it.

From ranking to understanding

Two systems can arrive at a similar overall score through very different strengths. A richer analysis can look at where control is reliable, where a scene drifts, and where apparent success depends on an easy setting.

The research direction is to organize these observations into interpretable capability profiles. The aim is a clearer account of behavior, without allowing a single aggregate to hide important differences.
