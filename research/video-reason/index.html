VideoReason: Understanding Events Across Time

A video is not a stack of images. It is a record of what changed.

Explore the idea ↓All research ↗

Concept Preview · This is a sample project with illustrative copy, created to preview the FudanCVL Robotics website.

Video understanding asks a model to connect evidence across time. Objects appear and disappear, actions unfold in stages, and the meaning of a moment often depends on what happened before it and what follows.

Follow the event, not only the frame

VideoReason is an illustrative research concept for studying entities, actions, and relationships across a sequence. The goal is to keep track of who did what, when it happened, and which parts of the scene changed as a result.

This framing treats temporal continuity as evidence. A model may recognize every visible object and still miss the event that connects them.

Connect perception with explanation

Useful video understanding goes beyond naming actions. It can ask why an outcome occurred, whether one event preceded another, and how a subject's goal is reflected in the sequence.

The concept brings recognition, tracking, and temporal reasoning into one view. It is a preview of a possible research direction, rather than a released model or a set of measured results.

Questions across time

01

Can a model maintain entity identity through occlusion and viewpoint change?

02

Does it distinguish correlation from a plausible causal sequence?

03

Can it explain an event using evidence from the relevant moments?

Understanding video means following change through time.
