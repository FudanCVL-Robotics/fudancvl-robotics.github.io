WorldTrace: Make An Interaction Easier To Understand

A score tells you where to look. A trace helps you see why.

Explore the idea ↓All research ↗

Concept Preview · This is a sample project with illustrative copy, created to preview the FudanCVL Robotics website.

On this pageOverviewPut the interaction back in orderKeep explanations close to evidenceQuestions for better research tools

When a model fails during a longer interaction, the interesting moment may happen well before the final output. Understanding that failure means connecting the request, the action, the generated observation, and the evidence used to evaluate it.

Put the interaction back in order

WorldTrace is an illustrative tooling concept for organizing a research run as a sequence of inspectable steps. Each step connects what was requested to what changed and what was observed afterward.

The aim is to make a result easier to discuss. A shared trace can point a researcher to a particular turn or transition instead of requiring them to reconstruct the entire interaction from separate files.

Keep explanations close to evidence

An evaluation is easier to assess when its observations remain connected to the outputs they describe. Side-by-side views of actions and outcomes could help researchers compare behaviors, inspect difficult cases, and identify recurring failure patterns.

This is a design concept for research tooling. The preview describes a possible workflow; it does not claim that a software package or an interactive trace service has been released.

Questions for better research tools

01

Can a reader locate the exact step behind an evaluation finding?

02

Are actions, observations, and annotations connected clearly?

03

Does the view help explain a difference between two model behaviors?

Make the path from observation to explanation visible.
